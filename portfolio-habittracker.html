About

Habit Streak Tracker is a local-first habit tracker built around GitHub-style calendar heatmaps. Habits can be daily or “N times per week”, each with a name, color, and target frequency. The Today view is a one-tap check-off list with live streak badges and weekly progress (e.g. 2/3 this week); the stats view shows a 12-month heatmap per habit, a combined all-habits heatmap with intensity scaled by daily activity, and weekly/monthly consistency percentages on a trend chart with a 12-week / 6-month toggle. Everything persists to localStorage — no account, no backend — with validated JSON export/import for backups.

The interesting engineering is in the date and streak logic, which lives as pure, dependency-free functions under src/lib/. All bookkeeping uses local YYYY-MM-DD date keys rather than UTC ISO strings, so a late-night check-off or a DST transition never shifts a day. Streaks handle the awkward cases deliberately: weekly-target habits count a week as satisfied once N checks are met, and the in-progress week never breaks a streak early. The heatmaps and trend charts are hand-rolled SVG — zero chart-library dependencies — and the whole UI follows prefers-color-scheme and stays responsive down to phone widths.

Because the tricky logic is pure functions, it's directly unit-testable: a Vitest suite covers streak gaps, in-progress weeks, DST transitions, and leap years — exactly the edge cases that silently corrupt streak counts in naive implementations.
