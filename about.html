About

I'm a software engineer and AI prompt engineer at the SIU School of Medicine, where I design, build, and operate SILOMed — a multi-tenant generative-AI medical simulation platform (React, Node.js, MySQL, Docker, AWS) that delivers AI patient encounters to medical students and residents. My day-to-day work spans custom Amazon Bedrock agents, agentic LLM workflows, prompt engineering, and secure enterprise integration (Microsoft Entra ID SSO, OAuth 2.0, multi-tenant data isolation). I'm a Boise State computer science graduate, currently completing an M.S. in Computer Science, and a patent co-inventor and invited national speaker on generative AI in medical education. In my free time I love working on my home server, building new projects, and learning new frameworks and languages.
